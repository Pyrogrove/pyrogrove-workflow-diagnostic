# Project Rules

## Classification

`PRACTICE BUILD — NOT CLAIMED AS EVENT-DAY WORK`

## Architecture

`LANE B — STREAMLIT CORE WITH MULERUN AUXILIARY`

The local Python repository is authoritative. MuleRun output is advisory until validated by deterministic rules.

## Deterministic authority

The following remain deterministic:

- required facts;
- qualification criteria;
- score;
- score bands;
- permitted routes;
- state transitions;
- clarification limit;
- one-revision limit;
- failure and rejection handling;
- human approval.

## Required facts

- workflow owner;
- primary user;
- trigger;
- input;
- output;
- frequency;
- consequence;
- current tools;
- at least one exception.

## Qualification criteria

Only `YES` scores one point:

1. repeated pain;
2. frequency;
3. volume;
4. named workflow owner;
5. business impact;
6. sample evidence;
7. budget signal;
8. timeline;
9. narrow MVP fit;
10. supportability.

Score bands:

```text
8–10 = MVP_CANDIDATE
5–7  = DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY
0–4  = NOT_QUALIFIED
```

Permitted routes:

```text
M365
RPA_DESKTOP
DATA_CODE
CODED_MICROAPP
NO_BUILD
```

## AI boundary

AI or MuleRun may extract, summarise, draft, recommend, challenge, and propose acceptance tests.

AI or MuleRun may not invent evidence, override status or score, approve, price, send externally, change business rules, create customer commitments, or claim production readiness.

## Explicit exclusions

- Hermes;
- SharePoint or Microsoft Graph;
- real email or client data;
- authentication or multi-tenancy;
- React, Next.js, FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL;
- n8n, RAG, MCP, vector databases;
- LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen;
- blockchain or crypto;
- local LLM;
- pricing;
- autonomous sending;
- customer or deployment commitments;
- production hosting;
- Vercel;
- Hermes VPS.

## Spending

New cash spending is `USD 0`.
